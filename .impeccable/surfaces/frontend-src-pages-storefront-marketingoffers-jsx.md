# MITTE Offers and Campaigns

- **Scope and mode:** Public German-language offer index and campaign detail pages; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** Shoppers comparing currently available promotions and deciding whether an offer fits their basket.
- **Action and proof:** Read the discount, minimum basket, automatic-application or coupon-code instructions, demo status, and campaign description; follow the offer CTA to the targeted product, category, or shop.
- **Constraints:** Render only campaign data returned by the public offers API. Keep loading, API failure, unavailable campaign, and no-active-offers states distinct. Demo prices and discounts must remain visibly labeled as simulated. Do not imply unverified product pricing, savings, popularity, stock, or campaign imagery is real.
- **Direction and memorable moment:** Inherit MITTE's Useful Find storefront identity. Lead with the active offer and its concrete benefit, then make eligibility and the next shopping action easy to scan; keep campaign cards image-led only when approved media exists.
- **Unresolved:** Production campaign copy, image rights and provenance, and verified commercial pricing must be confirmed before launch.
