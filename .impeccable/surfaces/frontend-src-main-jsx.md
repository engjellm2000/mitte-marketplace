# MITTE Storefront

- **Scope and mode:** Public German-language MITTE marketplace storefront; Persuade.
- **Audience and job:** Shoppers exploring a wide assortment across technology, home, beauty, fashion, sport, kitchen, garden, tools, family, office, pets, food, mobility, and travel.
- **Action and proof:** Search or browse nested categories, ask the Gemini shopping assistant to find and compare active catalog products, inspect catalog-backed price and availability, save an item, and add available products to the cart.
- **Constraints:** Preserve existing account, saved-items, cart, checkout, order, payment, seller, and legal routes. The assistant sends conversation messages to Google Gemini, does not persist chat history, and can only search the public catalog; shoppers make all cart changes through explicit product-card actions. Bestseller rankings must use confirmed paid or fulfilled sales. Personal history and recommendations require explicit account consent; withdrawal deletes stored views.
- **Direction and memorable moment:** Search-first marketplace with a crisp MITTE brand, navy trust cues, orange actions, broad category wayfinding, product cards shaped for fast comparison, and a docked chat assistant.
- **Unresolved:** Gemini API key, data-processing terms, privacy notice, and legal basis must be verified before enabling the assistant in production. Synthetic local catalog records and external placeholder imagery are not production evidence. Supplier data, commercial claims, product media, prices, and stock require verification before launch.
